About Dave

Dave Patton is a Realtor® and Certified Senior Housing Professional (CSHP) operating out of the greater Birmingham, Alabama area. He works under Patton Real Estate and KW Hoover (Keller Williams) from his office at 1021 Brocks Gap Pkwy, Hoover, AL.

Originally from Atlanta and a graduate of Oglethorpe University, Dave spent decades in consulting and television before transitioning to real estate — a path shaped by personal experience helping aging family members navigate one of life's most complex moves.

He serves the full residential market but his deepest expertise, certifications, and personal mission center on seniors and senior transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Certified Senior Housing Professional (CSHP)?

The CSHP designation is awarded by the Seniors Real Estate Institute to agents who complete intensive coursework and examinations in senior housing transitions, elder care coordination, and downsizing. Dave Patton is one of only two agents in the entire state of Alabama to hold this designation.

Does Dave only work with seniors?

No. Dave serves the full residential market across Hoover and greater Birmingham. His deepest expertise and certifications center on seniors and senior transitions, but he works with buyers and sellers at every stage of life.

What areas does Dave Patton serve?

Dave serves Hoover and greater Birmingham, Alabama — with hyper-local expertise in Lake Wilborn, Stadium Trace Village, Abingdon, and surrounding communities.

What brokerage does Dave Patton work with?

Dave operates under Patton Real Estate and KW Hoover (Keller Williams), at 1021 Brocks Gap Pkwy, Hoover, Alabama.

How does Dave help seniors and their families?

Dave guides seniors, adult children, and family caregivers through the real estate side of major life transitions — downsizing from a long-time home, coordinating timing with assisted living moves, managing estate property decisions — with patience, local expertise, and genuine care for the outcome.
